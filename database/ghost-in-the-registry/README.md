Diverted narcotics need a paper trail that leads nowhere. Investigators suspect a fabricated patient — one who is issued controlled-substance prescriptions but has never actually been seen for a single clinical encounter. Aurora uses one enterprise `patient_id` across all of its systems, so records join across databases. Find the patient who has at least one Schedule II prescription but zero encounters in `aurora_ehr`, and report their MRN.

Use provided MySQL shell.

Submit the MRN as `deadface{MRN}`. Example: `deadface{AH-1234567}`.
